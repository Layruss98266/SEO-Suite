About

Technical SEO,
hosted on your own terms.

We believe your websites, target keywords, and audit findings are your private business. While most SEO tools lock your data inside their own database, SEO Suite runs directly on your computer. You keep full control of your data, your settings, and your workspace.

Unifying the Toolchain

Doing technical SEO usually means bouncing between separate crawlers, schema generators, PageSpeed windows, API managers, and giant spreadsheets. We wanted to bring all of that into one fast, local dashboard. Now you can run deep site audits, verify actual Google indexation, analyze organic click-through rates, and write structured markup all in one place.

Zero-Markup Performance

SEO Suite is built on a fast, modern Python engine. It pairs a lightweight Flask interface with built-in security protections, automated browser scraping, and direct connections to official APIs like Google Search Console, PageSpeed, Moz, and SerpAPI. Everything runs directly on your hardware and saves reports straight to your local drive.
