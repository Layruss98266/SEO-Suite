About

Technical SEO,
hosted on your own terms.

A self-hosted SEO platform for the people who actually run the audits.

Who it's for

In-house SEOs, agencies, and developers who'd rather own their tooling than rent it. People who already pay for Search Console, PageSpeed, Moz, or SerpAPI access and don't want a second markup on top.

Why we built it

Most technical SEO work was scattered across separate crawlers, schema generators, PageSpeed tabs, and spreadsheets. We pulled the pieces that matter — crawling, indexation, Core Web Vitals, schema, Search Console — into one dashboard you run on your own host.

What we don't do

No SaaS account. No per-URL pricing. No telemetry. No vendor lock-in. Your audit data, API keys, and reports stay on your machine — and your contract with this tool is whatever the MIT licence says it is.

Principles

What we optimize for

🔒

Privacy First

Your data is yours. It stays on your device, we don't track your usage, and nothing gets shared with third parties.

✅

Accurate Signals

We rely on official data sources. We trust official Search Console APIs over third-party scraping, validate every schema, and keep outputs clean.

🧰

Practical Advice

You get clear scores and a prioritized list of action items with ready-to-use fixes, rather than a massive wall of confusing data.
