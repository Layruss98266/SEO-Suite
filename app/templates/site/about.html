About

SEO tooling that
respects your data.

Most SEO platforms send your sites, keywords, and traffic to someone else's cloud. SEO Suite runs wherever you put it: your laptop, your server, your rules.

Why it exists

Technical SEO usually means juggling crawlers, validators, APIs, and spreadsheets. SEO Suite brings the essentials together (audits, indexation, Core Web Vitals, structured data, and Search Console) into one local dashboard, so you can spend your time fixing issues instead of wiring tools together.

How it's built

A Flask backend with SSRF-safe fetching and rate limiting, a Playwright-driven indexer, and integrations with Google Search Console, PageSpeed, Moz, DataForSEO, and SerpAPI. It all runs in process and writes to your own disk.

Principles

What we optimize for

🔒

Privacy first

Your data stays on your machine. No telemetry, and nothing shared with third parties by default.

✅

Correctness

Authoritative sources come first. We trust Search Console over scraping, validate schema, and escape output.

🧰

Practicality

You get scored, prioritized findings and fixes you can paste in, not a pile of raw data.
