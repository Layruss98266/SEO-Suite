Crawl & Routing Checks

On-Page Optimization

Security & SSL Auditing

Live Indexation Checks

IndexNow & Bing Webmaster

Submit new or updated URLs directly to Bing and Yandex in seconds using the IndexNow protocol — no crawl queue wait needed. The Bing Webmaster integration also lets you inspect individual URLs, check traffic and crawl stats, and submit pages for re-crawl from inside the dashboard.

Tools & Diagnostics

🔎

Diagnostics — catch crawl traps before they bleed traffic.

Diagnostics — Robots, Hreflang & Links

Fetch any live robots.txt and get a plain-English breakdown of every directive, blocked path, and potential crawl trap. Validate hreflang tags on real pages and confirm alternate URLs respond. Probe every outbound link on a page to surface broken and redirected destinations before they hurt your authority.

🗺️

Sitemaps — stop Google quietly skipping your pages.

Sitemap Audit

Pull a live sitemap — including sitemap index files — and check every entry for duplicate URLs, oversized files, HTTP links that should be HTTPS, and malformed lastmod dates. Catches structural issues that cause Google to skip pages entirely.

Check page loading speeds using the official Google PageSpeed API. Compare your Core Web Vitals on mobile and desktop side-by-side to find exactly what is slowing you down.
